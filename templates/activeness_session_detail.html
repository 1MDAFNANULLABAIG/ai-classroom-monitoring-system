How to read this report. Every percentage below is a share of heuristic, camera-based checks made every ~1.5s during the session — e.g. "82% active" means the student looked engaged in 82% of the checks that had enough data, not that they were watched for 82% of the clock time. These signals (head turned away, eyes measured shut, a hand near the face) have real false positives — a stretch, a blink, or scratching an ear can all look similar to the camera. Treat this as a prompt to check in with a student, not as proof of what they were doing, and never as an automatic mark against them.
